SCC Para 34
Statutory Scope: Staggered Police Custody Under BNSS 2023
auto_stories (2024) 6 SCC 412 @ Page 438, Para 34 Author: Narasimha, J.
“We hold that the police custody of 15 days is not required to be continuous and may be availed in parts across the initial 40 or 60 days of the detention period under Section 187(3) of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023. The restrictive mandate enunciated in earlier decisions under Section 167 CrPC stands superseded by the explicit legislative variation incorporated by Parliament.”
